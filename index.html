I am currently a Ph.D. student at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), advised by Prof. Wenchao Xu. I received my B.Eng. degree in Electronic Information Engineering from Xidian University, advised by Prof. Nan Cheng and Xiucheng Wang.

My research focuses on embodied intelligence, network security, and AI for wireless systems, with applications in robotic manipulation, website fingerprinting, and RF fingerprint identification.

Preprint · 2026

World-to-Wrist: Task-Conditioned Future Wrist Modeling for Fine-Grained Robot Manipulation

Yuhao Pan*, Haosong Peng*, Zhengshen Zhang, Zhengyang Yan, Yalun Dai, Fushuo Huo, Chujie Wang, Tianyu Qi, Xiucheng Wang, Nan Cheng, Wenchao Xu

World-to-Wrist introduces task-conditioned future wrist modeling for multi-view vision-language-action systems. By anticipating how the robot’s wrist-camera observations will evolve before actions are executed, the model strengthens spatial grounding, long-horizon reasoning, and fine-grained manipulation in both simulation and real-world tasks.

SpatialBench: Is Your Spatial Foundation Model an All-Round Player?

Haosong Peng*, Hao Li*, Jiaqi Chen*, Yuhao Pan*, Runmao Yao, Yalun Dai, Fushuo Huo, Fangzhou Hong, Zhaoxi Chen, Haozhao Wang, Dingwen Zhang, Ziwei Liu, Wenchao Xu

SpatialBench provides a unified evaluation of spatial foundation models across 19 datasets, 546 scenes, six model paradigms, and five application domains. Its deterministic multi-density protocol enables reproducible comparisons and exposes practical gaps in long-sequence memory, out-of-domain generalization, and training-data quality.

PrismWF: A Multi-Granularity Patch-Based Transformer for Robust Website Fingerprinting Attack

Yuhao Pan, Wenchao Xu, Fushuo Huo, Haozhao Wang, Xiucheng Wang, Nan Cheng

PrismWF addresses multi-tab website fingerprinting by learning traffic patterns at multiple temporal granularities. Convolutional branches extract features with different receptive fields, while hierarchical interactions pass fine-grained details to coarser representations and use router tokens to exchange information within and across granularities. This design combines local traffic details with broader patterns to identify websites in mixed traces, including under website fingerprinting defenses.

IEEE TCCN · 2026

Cross-Receiver Generalization for RF Fingerprint Identification via Feature Disentanglement and Adversarial Training

Yuhao Pan, Xiucheng Wang, Fushuo Huo, Nan Cheng, Wenchao Xu

This work improves RF fingerprint identification when training and deployment use different receivers. The proposed framework disentangles transmitter and receiver characteristics and applies adversarial training to suppress receiver-specific artifacts while preserving identity cues that generalize to previously unseen hardware.

Xiucheng Wang, Yuhao Pan, Nan Cheng, Çağkan Yapar, Ruijin Sun, Zhisheng Yin, Conghao Zhou, Wenchao Xu, Yuxiang Zhang, Jianhua Zhang, Shuguang Cui, Xuemin Shen

Journal of Information and Intelligence · 2026

CRAFT: Complexity-Routed Attention with Frequency-Temporal Fusion for Multi-Tab Website Fingerprinting

Yuhao Pan, Fushuo Huo, Haosong Peng, Kang Wei

CRAFT addresses variations in traffic overlap during multi-tab browsing. It combines temporal features with frequency-domain patterns through learned gating, then uses a lightweight router to estimate the mixing complexity of each token and adapt its attention range. The model focuses on nearby context in simpler segments and expands its scope in heavily overlapping regions, capturing both traffic rhythms and changing dependencies.
