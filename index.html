Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)

I am currently a Ph.D. candidate at HKUST, advised by Prof. Wenchao Xu.

My research interests include Embodied Intelligence, Network Security, and AI for Wireless Systems. My recent work explores vision-language-action models for robot manipulation, radio-frequency intelligence, and website fingerprinting.
