About me

Yuhao Pan

I am currently a Ph.D. candidate in the Division of Integrative Systems and Design at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), advised by Prof. Wenchao Xu. I received my B.Eng. degree in Electronic Information Engineering from Xidian University.

My research interests include Embodied Intelligence, Network Security, and AI for Wireless Systems. My recent research spans vision-language-action models for robotic manipulation, radio-frequency intelligence, and website fingerprinting.

Preprint · 2026

World-to-Wrist: Task-Conditioned Future Wrist Modeling for Fine-Grained Robot Manipulation

Yuhao Pan*, Haosong Peng*, Zhengshen Zhang, Zhengyang Yan, Yalun Dai, Fushuo Huo, Chujie Wang, Tianyu Qi, Xiucheng Wang, Nan Cheng, Wenchao Xu

World-to-Wrist introduces task-conditioned future wrist modeling for multi-view vision-language-action systems. By anticipating how the robot’s wrist-camera observations will evolve before actions are executed, the model strengthens spatial grounding, long-horizon reasoning, and fine-grained manipulation in both simulation and real-world tasks.

NeurIPS · 2026

SpatialBench: Is Your Spatial Foundation Model an All-Round Player?

Haosong Peng*, Hao Li*, Jiaqi Chen*, Yuhao Pan*, Runmao Yao, Yalun Dai, Fushuo Huo, Fangzhou Hong, Zhaoxi Chen, Haozhao Wang, Dingwen Zhang, Ziwei Liu, Wenchao Xu

SpatialBench provides a unified evaluation of spatial foundation models across 19 datasets, 546 scenes, six model paradigms, and five application domains. Its deterministic multi-density protocol enables reproducible comparisons and exposes practical gaps in long-sequence memory, out-of-domain generalization, and training-data quality.

A Tutorial on Learning-Based Radio Map Construction: Data, Paradigms, and Physics-Awareness

Xiucheng Wang, Yuhao Pan, Nan Cheng, Çağkan Yapar, Ruijin Sun, Zhisheng Yin, Conghao Zhou, Wenchao Xu, Yuxiang Zhang, Jianhua Zhang, Shuguang Cui, Xuemin Shen

This tutorial organizes learning-based radio map construction into source-aware forward prediction and source-agnostic inverse reconstruction. It reviews representative datasets and learning paradigms, then explains how propagation physics and electromagnetic knowledge can improve accuracy, data efficiency, and generalization.

PrismWF: A Multi-Granularity Patch-Based Transformer for Robust Website Fingerprinting Attack

Yuhao Pan, Wenchao Xu, Fushuo Huo, Haozhao Wang, Xiucheng Wang, Nan Cheng

PrismWF targets robust website fingerprinting when multiple browsing activities are mixed within the same traffic trace. It combines multi-branch feature extraction with multi-granularity attention and router tokens, allowing the model to capture both local packet patterns and longer-range dependencies across overlapping traffic.

IEEE TCCN · 2026

Cross-Receiver Generalization for RF Fingerprint Identification via Feature Disentanglement and Adversarial Training

Yuhao Pan, Xiucheng Wang, Fushuo Huo, Nan Cheng, Wenchao Xu

This work improves RF fingerprint identification when training and deployment use different receivers. The proposed framework disentangles transmitter and receiver characteristics and applies adversarial training to suppress receiver-specific artifacts while preserving identity cues that generalize to previously unseen hardware.

CRAFT: Complexity-Routed Attention with Frequency-Temporal Fusion for Multi-Tab Website Fingerprinting

Yuhao Pan, Fushuo Huo, Haosong Peng, Kang Wei

CRAFT addresses website fingerprinting under realistic multi-tab traffic by jointly modeling temporal dynamics and frequency-domain structure. Its complexity-routed transformer adapts the attention range to each token, combining local and global evidence while keeping the model focused on the most informative traffic patterns.

GNN-Empowered Effective Partial Observation MARL Method for AoI Management in Multi-UAV Network

Yuhao Pan, Xiucheng Wang, Zhiyao Xu, Nan Cheng, Wenchao Xu, Jun-jie Zhang

This paper models dynamic relationships among UAVs with graph neural networks and integrates the learned representation into a multi-agent reinforcement learning framework. The approach enables distributed coordination under partial observations and improves age-of-information management as network topology and information freshness change over time.
